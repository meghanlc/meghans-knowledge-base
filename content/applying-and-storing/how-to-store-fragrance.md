# How to Store Fragrance

Proper storage can help protect a fragrance from conditions that may affect its quality over time. Where a fragrance is stored can be just as important as how it is used.

## Keep Fragrance Away From Heat

Fragrance should be stored away from excessive heat. High temperatures can affect the ingredients and may change the way a fragrance smells over time. A cool, stable environment is generally a better choice than a location that becomes very warm.

## Avoid Direct Sunlight

Direct sunlight can expose fragrance to heat and light. Keeping fragrance away from direct sunlight can help protect it.

A drawer, cabinet, or other shaded location can be useful for storage.

## Keep the Bottle Closed

Keeping the bottle or container properly closed when it is not being used can help reduce unnecessary exposure to air. Make sure the cap is securely replaced after using the fragrance.

## Choose a Stable Location

Frequent changes in temperature can also be undesirable for fragrance storage. A stable indoor location is generally preferable to places with large temperature changes.

For example, a bedroom drawer or cabinet may provide a more stable environment than a location near a window.
