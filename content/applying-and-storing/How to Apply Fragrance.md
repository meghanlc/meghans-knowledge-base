# How to Apply Fragrance

Applying fragrance is a simple part of a fragrance routine, and knowing how much fragrance to use and where to apply it can affect how noticeable the scent is.

## Start With a Small Amount

When applying a fragrance, it is usually helpful to start with a small amount. You can always add more if needed, but starting with less makes it easier to control how strong the fragrance smells.

## Apply to Skin

Fragrance is commonly applied to the skin. Many people choose areas such as the wrists or neck because these areas are easy to reach.

Avoid rubbing the fragrance into your skin after applying it, because rubbing can change how the fragrance is experienced and may cause the scent to fade faster.

## Give the Fragrance Time

Fragrances can change over time after they are applied. Different ingredients become noticeable at different stages, so allowing the fragrance to develop can help you understand how it smells.

This can also be useful when testing a new fragrance. Instead of deciding immediately, give the fragrance some time before making a judgment.

## Consider the Setting

The amount of fragrance you use can depend on where you are going. A fragrance that feels appropriate for one setting may be too noticeable in another. Being considerate of the people around you is an important part of wearing fragrance.

## Explore Your Preferences

There is no single way that everyone needs to apply fragrance. Experimenting with different application methods can help you discover what works best for you!
