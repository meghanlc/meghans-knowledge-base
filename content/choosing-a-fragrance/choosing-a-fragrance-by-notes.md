# Choosing a Fragrance by Notes

One way to choose a fragrance is by identifying the types of notes you enjoy. Fragrance notes describe the individual scents that contribute to the overall smell of a fragrance.

## Identify Your Favorite Notes

Think about scents you already enjoy. You may prefer fresh citrus scents, floral aromas, sweet fragrances, or warm woody notes.

- [Floral Notes](../fragrance-notes/floral-notes)

- [Woody Notes](../fragrance-notes/woody-notes)

Exploring these categories can help you recognize patterns in the fragrances you already like.

## Look at the Fragrance Description

When exploring a new fragrance, read its description and note list. The listed notes can provide an idea of the types of scents you may experience. It's important to keep in mind that a fragrance may smell different on different people, so the listed notes are a starting point rather than a guarantee of exactly how it will smell.

## Try Different Combinations

Fragrances often combine several types of notes. A fragrance might contain citrus and floral notes, for example, or combine sweet and woody characteristics.

Trying different combinations can help you discover new preferences.

## Keep Track of Your Preferences

Keeping a list of fragrances you enjoy can make it easier to identify patterns over time. Recording the notes you like can also help when looking for new fragrances and finding your new favorites!
