# Choosing a Fragrance for Different Occasions

Different situations may influence which fragrance someone chooses to wear. There are no strict rules for matching a fragrance to an occasion, but considering the setting can help when deciding between different scents.

## Everyday Wear

For everyday activities, some people prefer fragrances with fresh, clean, or lighter characteristics. Citrus and fresh notes can be options for someone who wants a simple scent for daily activities.

Explore [Citrus Notes](../fragrance-notes/citrus-notes/) and [Fresh Notes](../fragrance-notes/fresh-notes/) to learn more.

## Work or School

For work or school, a person may prefer a fragrance that is comfortable and appropriate for the setting. A lighter fragrance or one with subtle notes may be a personal preference. The choice can also depend on workplace or school expectations and the preferences of the people around you, especially because some people are senstive to certains smells.

## Special Events

Special events can provide an opportunity to explore different fragrance styles. Some people may choose floral, sweet, or woody fragrances depending on their personal preferences and the event.

Explore [Floral Notes](../fragrance-notes/floral-notes/), [Sweet Notes](../fragrance-notes/sweet-notes/), and [Woody Notes](../fragrance-notes/woody-notes/) for examples.

## Choosing What Feels Right

The most important part of choosing a fragrance is finding a scent that you enjoy and feel comfortable wearing. The same fragrance can work for multiple situations depending on the person wearing it.

Learning about [Fragrance Families](../fragrance-families/) and [Fragrance Concentrations](../fragrance-basics/fragrance-concentrations/) can help you understand your options when selecting a fragrance.
