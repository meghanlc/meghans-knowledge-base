# Floral Fragrance Family

The floral fragrance family includes scents inspired by flowers. Floral fragrances can range from light and delicate to rich and intense, depending on the flowers and other ingredients used.

## Common Floral Ingredients

Some common floral notes include rose, jasmine, lavender, and other flowers. Different flowers contribute different characteristics to a fragrance.

Floral scents can also be combined with other fragrance notes to create a wide variety of fragrance profiles. You can explore individual floral scents in [Floral Notes](../fragrance-notes/floral-notes.md/).

## Characteristics of Floral Fragrances

Floral fragrances are often described as:

- Soft

- Romantic

- Fresh

- Sweet

- Elegant

- Delicate

These characteristics can vary depending on the specific ingredients and the other notes included in the fragrance.

## Floral Fragrances and Other Notes

Floral ingredients are frequently combined with other types of notes. For example, floral scents can be paired with citrus notes to create a bright fragrance or with woody notes to create a deeper scent.

Explore related scents through [Citrus Notes](../fragrance-notes/citrus-notes.md/) and [Woody Notes](../fragrance-notes/woody-notes.md/).

## Choosing a Floral Fragrance

People who enjoy floral scents have many options to explore because different flowers create very different fragrance experiences. Understanding the floral fragrance family can also make it easier to compare fragrances and recognize similarities between different products.
