# Citrus Fragrance Family

The citrus fragrance family is associated with bright, fresh, and often refreshing scents. Citrus fragrances commonly feature ingredients from fruits such as lemon, orange, grapefruit, lime, and bergamot.

## Common Citrus Ingredients

Citrus fragrances can include a variety of fruit-based notes. Lemon and lime often provide sharp, fresh qualities, while orange can create a sweeter citrus character. Grapefruit can add a slightly tart quality, and bergamot is commonly used to add a fresh citrus character to fragrances.

You can explore these individual scents in [Citrus Notes](../fragrance-notes/citrus-notes/).

## Characteristics of Citrus Fragrances

Citrus fragrances are often described as:

- Fresh

- Bright

- Clean

- Light

- Refreshing

- Energetic

The overall experience can change depending on which other notes are combined with the citrus ingredients.

## Citrus and Other Fragrance Families

Citrus notes can be combined with many other types of scents. For example, citrus can be paired with floral notes for a lighter and more delicate fragrance or with woody notes for a warmer and more complex scent.

You can explore these related categories through [Floral Notes](../fragrance-notes/floral-notes/) and [Woody Notes](../fragrance-notes/woody-notes/).

## Choosing a Citrus Fragrance

Someone who enjoys fresh or bright scents may be interested in exploring citrus fragrances. Learning about individual notes can make it easier to understand why different citrus fragrances smell different even when they share similar ingredients.
