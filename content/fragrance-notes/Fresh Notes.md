
## What Exactly Are Fresh Notes?

Fresh notes are clean, bright, and cooling. They evoke a sense of outdoor air, water, or even fresh citrus. It's a very clean and energizing note that pairs well with citrusy and floral scents. They help mimic aquatic vibes by using sea salt and rain, and can also bring a feeling of airy and just washed.

### Common Fresh Notes

- Green Tea
- Grass
- Mint
- Cucumber
- Aquatic
- Musk
- Linen

## How Fresh Notes Smell

- oceanic
- clean laundry
- garden after rain
- sea salt
- citrusy
- crisp
- clean
- cooling
- energetic
## Fresh Notes in Perfume

> Fresh notes in perfume can give a fragrance a clean and refreshing vibe. They are often used to make a perfume smell like you just got out of the shower.
