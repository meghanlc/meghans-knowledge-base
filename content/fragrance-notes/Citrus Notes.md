## What Exactly Are Citrus Notes?

Citrus notes are bright, uplifting, and fresh scents from predominantly the use of citrus fruits or the peels of citrus fruits. They can bring a lot to the overall scent of a perfume, and can greatly vary from sweet and juicy, to bitter and spicy. Notes of citrus are usually smelt right off the bat with it being such a strong smell. Citrus pairs well with scents like floral and can create a bright and soft fragrance. 

### Common Citrus Notes

- Lemon
- Orange
- Grapefruit
- Lime
- Bergamot

# How Citrus Notes Smell

- refreshing
- bright
- juicy
- clean
- zesty
- tart

## Citrus Notes in Perfume

> Having notes of citrus in a perfume gives the overall smell a bright and uplifting feeling, and gives off a refreshing vibe.
