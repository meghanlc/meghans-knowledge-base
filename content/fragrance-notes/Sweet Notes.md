## What Exactly Are Sweet Notes?

Sweet notes usually have a warm, and calming scent. This scent evokes a sugary, and rich feeling, sometimes it almost smells edible. They can be warm like vanilla, or deep and rich like chocolate. 
### Common Sweet notes

- Vanilla
- Caramel
- Chocolate
- Honey
- Fruit
- Cotton Candy
- Amber

## How Sweet notes Smell

- edible
- juicy
- warm
- bright
- deep
- sophisticated
- sugary

## Sweet Notes in Perfume

> Sweets notes in a perfume can make a fragrance feel comforting, and inviting. They are often used to make an overall scent richer.
