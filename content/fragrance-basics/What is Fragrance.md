# What Is Fragrance?

Fragrance is a scented product designed to give your skin, and clothing a pleasant smell based off of the vibe that you're going for. Fragrances can be made from a combination of natural and synthetic ingredients that create different scents and experiences.

## How Fragrance Is Made

A fragrance is created by combining different aromatic ingredients, and these ingredients can include essential oils, plant extracts, natural materials, and synthetic aroma compounds. Perfumers carefully combine these materials to create a specific scent.

Fragrances are often described using different **notes**. Notes are the individual scents that contribute to the overall fragrance. Some common types include citrus, floral, fresh, sweet, and woody notes.

## Fragrance Notes

Fragrance notes help describe what a fragrance smells like. For example, citrus notes can provide bright and refreshing scents, while woody notes can create warmer and deeper scents.

You can explore different types of notes in the [Fragrance Notes](../fragrance-notes/) section of this knowledge base.

## Fragrance Families

Fragrances can also be organized into **fragrance families**. A fragrance family groups scents together based on their general characteristics. Common families include floral, citrus, woody, fresh, and other scent categories.

Learn more about how fragrances are organized by visiting the [Fragrance Families](../fragrance-families/) page.

## Why Fragrance Is Personal

People can experience the same fragrance differently. Personal preferences, memories, and experiences can influence which scents someone enjoys. This is one reason there are so many different fragrance options available.

Fragrance can also be chosen based on the season, occasion, or the type of scent a person prefers.
