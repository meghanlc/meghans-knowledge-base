# Fragrance Ingredients

Fragrances are made by combining different ingredients that create their overall scent. These ingredients can come from natural sources, such as plants, flowers, fruits, and woods, or they can be created synthetically.

## Natural Ingredients

Natural fragrance ingredients can come from many different parts of plants. Flowers, fruits, leaves, roots, seeds, and woods can all provide aromatic materials.

For example, citrus fruits can provide bright and fresh scents, while flowers can contribute soft or floral aromas. Woody materials can create warmer and deeper scents.

Explore examples of these ingredients in [Citrus Notes](../fragrance-notes/citrus-notes/) and [Floral Notes](../fragrance-notes/floral-notes/).

## Synthetic Ingredients

Synthetic aroma compounds are created to produce specific scents. They can be used to recreate scents found in nature or to create aromas that are difficult to obtain from natural materials.

Synthetic ingredients also allow perfumers to create consistent fragrances and develop a wide range of different scent profiles.

## Essential Oils

Essential oils are concentrated aromatic materials commonly obtained from plants. They can contribute distinctive scents to fragrances and are used in many types of scented products.

Different essential oils can provide different characteristics. For example, citrus oils can create fresh and bright aromas, while some woody materials can create warmer scents.

## Combining Ingredients

Perfumers combine different ingredients to create a balanced fragrance, and the ingredients work together to produce the overall scent rather than being experienced as completely separate materials.

The combination of ingredients is what allows fragrances to have different personalities, from fresh and light to sweet, floral, or woody.
