# Fragrance Concentrations

Fragrance concentration refers to the amount of aromatic ingredients used in a fragrance compared with the amount of alcohol or other carrier ingredients. Different concentrations can affect how strong a fragrance smells and how long its scent may last.

## Eau de Cologne

Eau de Cologne generally has a lower concentration of aromatic ingredients. It is often associated with lighter and refreshing fragrances.

## Eau de Toilette

Eau de Toilette typically has a moderate concentration of aromatic ingredients. These fragrances are often used as everyday scents because they can provide a noticeable fragrance without being as concentrated as some other types.

## Eau de Parfum

Eau de Parfum generally contains a higher concentration of aromatic ingredients than Eau de Toilette. This can make the fragrance feel richer or more noticeable and may allow the scent to last longer.

## Parfum

Parfum generally has one of the highest concentrations of aromatic ingredients. Because of its higher concentration, only a small amount may be needed when applying it.

## Choosing a Concentration

The best concentration depends on personal preference and how someone plans to use the fragrance. A lighter concentration may be preferred for most everyday situations, while a more concentrated fragrance may be chosen when someone wants a stronger scent.

Concentration is only one part of choosing a fragrance. The fragrance's individual notes and overall fragrance family can also influence how it smells.
